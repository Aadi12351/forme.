# Forme — Premium Minimal Streetwear

Positioning: Everyday Essentials. Elevated.
Audience: fashion-conscious people building an everyday oversized/drop-shoulder wardrobe.

## Conversion path

Homepage/editorial campaign → relevant collection → clear product photography, colour and size → bag → transparent delivered total → guest checkout → verified order → dispatch/tracking → eligible support/returns.

Avoid fake testimonials, scarcity counters, unverified claims and automatic discount popups. The sample review section was removed. Campaign artwork is identified separately from product evidence.

## Photography plan

For each colour: real front and back images, neckline, seams and hem, on-body front/side/back with model height and worn size, a movement clip and packaged product. Record measured garment sizes and verified composition/GSM. AI/editorial imagery may establish a mood but cannot prove construction or actual fabric quality.

Use consistent light, neutral backgrounds and truthful colour. Export WebP/AVIF variants and retain originals. The supplied hero PNGs have WebP versions; the existing product photos now have WebP copies and responsive variants. Original photos are retained. A real garment/photo-accuracy audit remains necessary.

## Launch sequence

Week 1: verify garments, size charts, costs, inventory and delivered margin. Test the complete order and returns journey.
Week 2: publish a fit video, three-way styling video, real detail close-ups, colour carousel and founder story. Link directly to the relevant product/collection.
Week 3: after checkout is validated, test a small capped campaign with fit-led versus detail-led creative. Agree a stop-loss acquisition cost before spending.
Week 4: evaluate delivered contribution, exchange rate by size, returns-to-origin and repeat orders before scaling. Request authentic reviews from delivered buyers.

## Offers and economics

Contribution before advertising = net item revenue minus garment/print/packaging, gateway fees, seller-funded courier cost and expected returns.
The admin's product contribution metric is revenue minus product cost, not net profit; deduct courier/gateway/returns/overheads before setting an ad budget.
Rs 79 shipping/free above Rs 999 is the initial customer rule, not an assertion of courier cost. Consider multi-tee bundles only when contribution supports them.

## Measurement

Admin settings accept GA4 and Meta Pixel IDs. Tracking loads after optional consent. The app records consented views/add-to-cart/checkout events and counts verified live purchases from order data. CAPI is prepared behind server credentials and shares the purchase event ID with the browser.

Use consistent UTM source/medium/campaign names. Traffic sources in the built-in dashboard reflect consented events, not total visitors. Compare campaign cost against delivered margin. No ad campaign, Meta catalogue, Search Console verification or Instagram account has been created.

## Email / WhatsApp

Transactional order notifications are separated from marketing. Optional WhatsApp order updates require checkout opt-in and approved templates. Abandoned-checkout copy is provided as a disabled template only; do not run it without appropriate marketing permission, suppression and unsubscribe handling.
